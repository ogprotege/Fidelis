# Fidelis Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 13, 2026 · **Updated:** July 16, 2026 (device-backup
disclosure)

Fidelis is a Catholic Bible app built on a pledge: no accounts, no ads, no
tracking, ever. This policy is short because there is almost nothing to
disclose.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Fidelis has no server. The app makes no network requests of its
own, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and
no crash-reporting service. We never see your reading, your searches, your
notes, or your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything the app knows is stored on your device:

- **Settings** (theme, text size, translation choice, calendar region) —
  stored locally.
- **Bookmarks, highlights, and notes** — stored locally; export is under your
  control.
- **Imported translations** (e.g., a licensed NABRE, RSV-2CE, or Biblia
  Platense you own) and an **imported Catechism** — stored locally on your
  device and never transmitted anywhere by Fidelis.

## Device backups

Like any app's data, Fidelis's data can be included in **your own device
backup** — iCloud or computer backups on iOS, Google backup on Android —
according to your device's backup settings. Those backups belong to you and
are encrypted by Apple or Google; Fidelis never sees them, and nothing about
this involves any Fidelis server (there is none). If you restore a backup,
your settings, marginalia, and imported texts return with it.

Deleting the app deletes everything Fidelis stores on the device itself.
Copies inside your own device backups remain yours, governed by your
device's backup settings, until those backups are replaced or deleted. If
you prefer that no copy exist anywhere, exclude the app from your device
backup (or disable backup) before deleting it.

## Permissions

- **Photo library (add-only):** requested only if you tap "Save image" on a
  Scripture share card, and used only to save that image. The app cannot read
  your photos.

## Sharing and external links

- The **share card** and **Save image** use your device's system share sheet;
  where the content goes is your choice, through your OS.
- Links to the Catechism open the official text on **vatican.va** in your
  browser. Support links open **github.com**. Those sites have their own
  policies; Fidelis sends them nothing beyond the page request your browser
  makes.

## Home-screen widgets and Siri

The Verse of the Day, Mass readings, and Quote widgets — and the "today's
Gospel" Siri shortcut — read data bundled with the app on your device. No
network is involved.

## Children

Fidelis is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). Since the app collects no data
from anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy will be
updated in the repository before that version ships, with the change stated
plainly.
